WORKING RULES

How I operate when we work together

📋
Brief before action
Every engagement starts with a written brief — goals, audience, success metrics, constraints. I write it, you review it. No brief, no work.
🗓
Weekly sync or async update
For projects over 2 weeks, I commit to either a 30-min weekly call or a written progress update. You choose the format.
💬
One channel per project
Pick one: email or WeChat. Not both. I won't chase messages across platforms, and I'll remind you if the channel switches without notice.
🔄
Two rounds of revisions included
All deliverables include two revision rounds. Additional rounds are scoped and priced separately. This keeps both of us honest about the brief.
⚡
No urgent = no premium
Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days for most deliverables. Rush requests (under 48 hours) carry a 50% premium, no exceptions.
🤝
Feedback is welcome, direction is not
Tell me if something isn't working and why. I'll figure out how to fix it. Prescriptive direction ("just change this word") isn't how I work best.
